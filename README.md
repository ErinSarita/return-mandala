# The Return Mandala

A map for returning to your own earliest story and to the passages of growing up: the child you were at the center, ringed by the twelve questions of your beginning, and around them seven developmental rights, each read from the need to the belief it opens into, with lenses for attachment and the body, and a guide to finding a guide.

A companion to [The Pregnancy and Birth Mandala](https://erinsarita.github.io/prenatal-mandala/), turned around. That mandala keeps the baby at the center so a mother, a partner, and an educator can welcome her well. This one is for the adult that baby grew into: anyone, pregnant or not, who has noticed imprints in themselves and wants to go back, understand them, and meet the child they were with care.

Created by Erin Singleton, a student in APPPAH's Prenatal & Perinatal Educator certification. An educational map for reflection, not therapy or medical advice.

The mandala is the single file `index.html`. It began as a copy of the Pregnancy and Birth Mandala and keeps its drawing style, its braid, and its twelve questions, but the wheel itself is rebuilt around the passages of growing up:

- **The center:** the Seed of Life, ringed by the twelve questions of your beginning, from conception through the first hour, named by the need each one asks about (Wanted, Welcome, Safety, and so on). They are the core first imprint, the earliest layer of the Right to Exist, and the same twelve as the original mandala.
- **Around the wheel:** seven rights, after Robin Grille's developmental rites of passage (*Parenting for a Peaceful World*; *Inner Child Journeys*, 2019): to exist, to need, to have support, to freedom, to love, to playful learning, and to an opinion, a vocation, and sexual autonomy. Grille's model is described in our own words, not copied.
- **Center out, for each right:** the need, the imprint and the decision it can leave, how it can show in you now, healing nutrients, and the belief it opens into (the outer petals).
- **Each right's panel adds:** gathering the theme across a life, and counteraction and proaction (William Emerson, "Somatotropic Therapy," 2002); what tips the scale (Harvard's Center on the Developing Child, Working Paper 13, 2015); practices with why each helps (Candace Pert, *Molecules of Emotion*, 1997); and when to go deeper.
- **Tabs:** How to read, Your beginning, The seven rights, Attachment, The body (implicit memory, the window of tolerance, the ladder of states, stress and resilience, the chemistry of feeling, how to walk the wheel), and Your guide (educators and practitioners, where to look, and questions to ask).
- **The Pause:** a check-in (too much, shut down, okay) with what helps from each, plus grounding and crisis numbers.
- **Your birthday:** counts the full moons of your time in the womb and names the moon you were born under; with a due date, each station of Your beginning shows its dates.

The content lives in constants near the top of the script: `RIGHTS` and `WHEEL_TEXT` for the seven rights, `STATIONS`, `GROUPS`, `CHILD`, `YOU`, `GUIDE`, `REPAIR`, `QUESTIONS`, `NOW_Q`, `CIRCLE`, `PRACTICE`, and `HEALING` for your beginning, and `ATTACH`, `CHECKIN`, `BODY_HTML`, and `GUIDE_HTML` for the tabs. `QUESTIONS` is the same twelve as the Pregnancy and Birth Mandala; keep the two in step.

Everything a visitor enters stays in their own browser under `rm-` keys, kept apart from the Pregnancy and Birth Mandala's `pm-` keys because the two are served from the same site.
