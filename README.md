# The Return Mandala

A map for returning to your own earliest story, read from the center out: the child you were, you now, your circle of support, the guide, and practices for healing, with an attachment lens beside it.

A companion to [The Pregnancy and Birth Mandala](https://erinsarita.github.io/prenatal-mandala/), turned around. That mandala keeps the baby at the center so a mother, a partner, and an educator can welcome her well. This one is for the adult that baby grew into: anyone, pregnant or not, who has noticed imprints in themselves and wants to go back, understand them, and meet the child they were with care.

Created by Erin Singleton, a student in APPPAH's Prenatal & Perinatal Educator certification. An educational map for reflection, not therapy or medical advice.

The mandala is the single file `index.html`. It began as a copy of the Pregnancy and Birth Mandala's `index.html` and keeps its drawing, its twelve months, and its twelve questions, so the two can be read side by side. What changed:

- **The rings.** The child you were, You now (how each month may echo in you, in the body, and questions for exploring your story), Your circle of support, The guide (the possible imprint, the child's question and how it can sound now, repair words, and the attachment link), and Healing practices.
- **The Attachment tab.** The four adult styles on the two dimensions of anxiety and avoidance, each with the months the field links to it, a reflection, and earned security.
- **The Pause.** A grounding page one tap away in the header, with where to turn if it is too much.
- **The time wheel.** It takes your birthday, and optionally your due date, how your birth began, when labor began, and the time you were born, then marks the moons of your own time in the womb and the moon you were born under.

The month content lives in the constants near the top of the script: `CHILD`, `YOU`, `CIRCLE`, `GUIDE`, `REPAIR`, `QUESTIONS` and `NOW_Q`, `PRACTICE`, `HEALING`, and `ATTACH`. `QUESTIONS` is the same twelve as the Pregnancy and Birth Mandala; keep the two in step.

Everything a visitor enters stays in their own browser under `rm-` keys, kept apart from the Pregnancy and Birth Mandala's `pm-` keys because the two may be served from the same site.
